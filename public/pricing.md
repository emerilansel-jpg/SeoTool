## Credit System & Rate Card

Credits work like prepaid mobile balance: 1,000 credits = $1.00 USD. Your balance only deducts when actions run, and unused credits roll over every month without expiring.

### BYOK Mode (Bring Your Own Key)
For high-volume marketing agencies and enterprise users, BYOK mode allows connecting personal DataForSEO and OpenRouter API keys. Queries run with zero platform data markup and direct provider billing, allowing unlimited operations without credit caps.
