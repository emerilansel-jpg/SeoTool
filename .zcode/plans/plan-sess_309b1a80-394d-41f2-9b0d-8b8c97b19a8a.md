Mempertahankan design system cream/charcoal/orange (`.itc` scope). Restrukturisasi urutan section agar fitur tampil di depan, perluas feature grid ke SEMUA fitur yang dijual, tambah pricing teaser.

### Urutan section baru:

1. **Hero** (refine copy) — headline + subhead yang menyebutkan semua kategori fitur + CTA "Start enjoying SEO"
2. **Trust bar** (BARU, ringan) — satu baris: "The open-source alternative to Ahrefs and Semrush" + badge DataForSEO / GitHub stars / Product Hunt
3. **Feature grid prominen** (BARU, menggantikan product section yang terkubur) — 3 grup dengan heading:
   - **Research & Discovery:** Keyword Research, Domain Overview, Backlinks, Saved Keywords
   - **Monitor & Audit:** Rank Tracking, Site Audit, GSC Insights, GA4 Insights
   - **AI & Content:** AI Brand Visibility, Prompt Explorer, Content Intelligence, White-label Reports
   - Kartu yang punya halaman `/features/*` (8 dari featurePages) tetap link; 4 fitur baru (GSC/GA4/Content Intel/Reports) jadi kartu info yang link ke `/features`
4. **MCP section** (PERTAHANKAN — diferensiator unik) — terminal demo + ikon AI client, tetap "the one orange moment"
5. **Pricing teaser** (BARU) — 4 mini-card (Free/$0, Lite/$49, Pro/$149, Agency/$499) + headline "Transparent pricing that scales" + link ke `/pricing`
6. **Testimonials** (PERTAHANKAN)
7. **Open source** (PERTAHANKAN)
8. **Footer** (PERTAHANKAN)

### CSS additions ke `landing-page.css`:

- `.itc-trustbar` — baris trust signals
- `.itc-feature-group` + `.itc-feature-group-title` — grouping untuk feature grid 3-grup
- `.itc-pricing-teaser` + `.itc-pricing-card` — row pricing mini-cards (reuse pola `.itc-card`)
