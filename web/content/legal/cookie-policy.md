## Overview
This Cookie Policy explains how Every App, Inc. ("SeoTool.im", "we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website (seotool.im) and use our SaaS application. 

## What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device. We use them to make our application work, ensure security, and understand how people use our service.

## Types of Cookies We Use

**1. Essential Cookies**
We use strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in. These are required for the application to function securely.

**2. Analytics Cookies**
For our public website, we use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics. We do not track you across the internet. For the authenticated dashboard, we use internal analytics to improve our product features, which you can opt out of in your account settings.

## Managing Your Preferences
Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies. If you block essential cookies, you will not be able to sign in or use the SeoTool.im dashboard.

For questions about this policy, contact us at support@seotool.im.
